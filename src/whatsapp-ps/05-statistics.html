Statistics

Open the Statistics tab to see clicks from the last 90 days.

A click is counted when a shopper clicks a WhatsApp link. No personal data and no phone numbers of shoppers are stored.

  • Clicks in the last 90 days: the total for each kind of button.
  • Chats per agent: chats started for each agent.
  • Last 30 days with clicks: a table of clicks per day.

If there are no clicks yet, the table stays empty until shoppers use the buttons.

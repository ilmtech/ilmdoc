Product page buttons

Open the Product page tab. You can turn on four buttons for your product pages.

3.1 Order on WhatsApp

  • Show an Order on WhatsApp button: adds the button to product pages. The message has the chosen combination, quantity and price at the moment of the click.
  • WhatsApp number for orders: empty means the first agent.
  • Button text: up to 60 characters.
  • Message: up to 1000 characters.

You can use these variables in the message: {product_title} {variant} {product_reference} {quantity} {price} {product_url} {shop_name}.

3.2 Ask about this product

  • Show an Ask about this product button: adds the button to product pages.
  • WhatsApp number: empty means the first agent.
  • Button text: up to 60 characters.
  • Message: up to 1000 characters.
  • Only for products in these categories: empty means every product.
  • Show the icon: shows or hides the icon on the button.
  • CSS classes (optional, replaces the built-in style): your own button style.

3.3 Share on WhatsApp

  • Show a Share on WhatsApp button: the shopper chooses the contact. The message has the product name and link.
  • Button text: up to 60 characters.
  • Add the short description: adds the product short description to the message.
  • Only for products in these categories: empty means every product.
  • Show the icon: shows or hides the icon on the button.
  • CSS classes (optional): your own button style.

3.4 Subscribe for updates

  • Show a Subscribe button: adds the button to product pages.
  • The button opens: A chat with your number, or A link (WhatsApp channel or group invite).
  • WhatsApp number: empty means the first agent.
  • Link: the channel or group link.
  • Button text: up to 60 characters.
  • Message: up to 1000 characters.
  • Only for products in these categories: empty means every product.
  • Show the icon: shows or hides the icon on the button.
  • CSS classes (optional): your own button style.

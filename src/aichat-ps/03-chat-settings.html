Chat settings

3.1 Chat

  • Show the chat: switches the chat on in your shop.
  • Title: shown at the top of the chat window.
  • Welcome message: the first message. On product and category pages the welcome names the product or category.
  • Questions for each page: on product and category pages the AI writes four suggested questions once (one small request per page); they are kept and shown to every visitor of that page.
  • Suggested questions (other pages): one per line, up to four, for the home page and the other pages.
  • Invitation after (seconds): on product and category pages a small "Questions about…?" bubble appears after this many seconds, once per visit. 0 = never.
  • Position (bottom right or bottom left) and Colour of the chat.
  • Product cards: a card with picture, price and View button for each product an answer recommends.
  • Order questions: signed-in customers can ask about their own orders. Their last five orders (reference, date, status, products) are sent to the AI with their question.

3.2 When the assistant cannot help

When the AI does not know the answer, or the shopper asks for a person, reports a problem or is unhappy, the chat shows your contact options.

  • Message form: offers Leave a message. The visitor's e-mail, message and recent chat are e-mailed to the E-mail address below.
  • Contact page: the page the Contact form button opens.
  • WhatsApp number: with country code, e.g. 919876543210. Use a number that has the WhatsApp app (not a Cloud API number). Leave empty to hide the button.
  • Privacy page: linked under the chat (see Statistics and privacy).

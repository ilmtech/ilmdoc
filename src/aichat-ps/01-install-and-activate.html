Install and activate

1.1 Install the module

  1. In your back office, go to Modules > Module Manager and click Upload a module.
  2. Choose the file ilm-ai-chat.zip from your order page on ilmtech.in.
  3. When the upload is done, click Configure.

1.2 Activate your licence

Until the licence is active, the settings page shows only the licence bar and the chat is not shown in your shop.

  1. In the licence bar, type the Order ID of your purchase: the order reference on ilmtech.in (in your order e-mail and under My account > Order history).
  2. Click Activate. The bar then shows the order, your shop address and, with the support add-on, the date until which you get updates and support.
  • One licence is for one shop address.
  • When your shop moves to a new domain, the licence bar offers to move the licence there.
  • Remove licence from this shop frees the licence for another shop.

1.3 Switch the chat on

In the Chat section, set Show the chat to Yes and click Save. Choose your AI first (see AI provider): the chat is shown only when the AI can answer.

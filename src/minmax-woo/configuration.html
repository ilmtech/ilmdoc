The Configuration tab has the sub-tabs General and Messages, and Advanced with the Pro add-on. On General, each switch adds its own column to the Quantity/Amount Rule table, and the value next to it is the shop-wide (Global) default, used until a more specific rule overrides it.

SettingWhat it does
Minimum QuantityCustomers must order at least this many units of a product.
Maximum QuantityCustomers cannot order more than this many units of a product.
Multiples of QuantityCustomers must order in packs of this size, e.g. 3, 6, 9 with a value of 3.
Limit Multiple QuantityAbove this quantity, the pack rule stops applying and single units are allowed again.
Start from the minimumPart of Multiples of Quantity. Enabled: steps count from the Minimum Quantity, so a minimum of 5 with steps of 2 allows 5, 7, 9… Disabled: 6, 8, 10 (multiples of 2 from the minimum up).
Minimum AmountCustomers must spend at least this much on the product (or on the category, brand, tag or user role, for those levels).
Maximum AmountCustomers cannot spend more than this much. Amount and quantity limits can be used together on the same rule.
Amounts include taxMeasures Minimum and Maximum Amount with tax, in the cart and in past orders. Off: without tax.
Allowed QuantitiesAdds the Allowed Qty column. Its Global value (e.g. 6,12,24) is the Global rule's value, used like the other Global values when no more specific rule matches.
Different products per orderHow many different products one order must hold (Minimum) and may hold (Maximum), whatever their quantities; 0 means no limit. Per variation: two variations of one product count as two.
Auto SaveSaves a change to any field in the rule table straight away, with no Save click.

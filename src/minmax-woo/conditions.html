Click Conditions on a row to apply the rule only when the cart meets every condition you set. When the conditions are not met, the rule is skipped and the next rule in line applies, on the product page as well as in the cart.

ConditionThe rule applies when…
Product pricethe price of one unit is within the range, with or without tax as set by Amounts include tax.
Stockthe product's (or variation's) stock is within the range.
Product attributes (any one)the product has at least one of the selected attribute values.
Countries / Shipping zonesthe customer is in one of the selected countries or shipping zones.
Languages / Currenciesthe shopper uses one of the selected languages or currencies.
Customer historyany customer, new customers (no paid order yet) or returning customers.
Last order within (days)the customer's last order is at most this many days old.

Leave a list empty to accept any value. Hold Ctrl (Cmd on Mac) to pick several entries.

Step by step

  1. The Art category has a minimum of three, for every product in it.

  2. Click Add in the Conditions column. The rule then only applies when the cart meets every condition you set.

  3. You can limit it to a product price range, or a stock range.

  4. To products with some attributes, or to delivery countries and zones.

  5. To new or returning customers, or to customers whose last order is recent.

  6. We type ten as the highest price. Leave a field empty to accept any value.

  7. Click Save. The button now shows one condition.

  8. The framed poster costs twenty nine dollars, more than ten, so there is no minimum for it.

  9. The vector print costs nine dollars, so the minimum of three applies. When the conditions are not met, the next rule in line applies instead.

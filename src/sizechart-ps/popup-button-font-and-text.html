• Button — Basic: the button title (per language; empty shows the icon only), direction (horizontal, vertical, or a custom angle), alignment and location: at the module's hook position, or next to a page element given by a CSS selector, before or after it. Margin and padding. Text: font size, font family (theme font or Google Fonts), decoration, transform, letter spacing and line height.

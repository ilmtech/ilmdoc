Languages

5.1 Translate your texts

This page works when your store has more than one language. Add languages in Settings → Languages in your Shopify admin.

  1. Pick a language.
  2. Fill in the texts you want to change.
  3. Click Save.

Empty fields show the text of your main language. Keep placeholders like {product_title} as they are.

5.2 What you can translate

  • Widget button text: the text on the chat button.
  • Chat window title: the title of the chat window.
  • Chat window text: the text in the chat window.
  • Offline message: the message outside business hours.
  • Greeting: the greeting bubble.
  • Ask about this product — button and Ask about this product — message: the question button.
  • Share — button: the share button.
  • Subscribe — button and Subscribe — message: the subscribe button.
  • Order (product) — button and Order (product) — message: the product order button.
  • Order (cart) — button and Order (cart) — message: the cart order button.
Tip If your store has one language, the texts you set on the other pages are used.

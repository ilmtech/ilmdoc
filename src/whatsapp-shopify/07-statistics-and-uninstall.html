Statistics and uninstall

7.1 Statistics

Open the Statistics page. Pick a period: Last 7 days, Last 30 days or Last 90 days.

The page counts clicks that open WhatsApp. It is anonymous. No names, numbers or messages are stored.

  • By button: Chat with an agent, Order on WhatsApp (product), Order on WhatsApp (cart), Ask about this product, Share, Subscribe and Chat window opened.
  • By agent: clicks per agent.
  • By page: clicks per page, such as Home, Product, Collection, Cart, Search, Blog, Pages and Other.

The Home page also shows the last 30 days: WhatsApp chats started, Order on WhatsApp clicks and Chat window opened.

7.2 Uninstall

You can uninstall the app from your Shopify admin. Your settings are kept. If you install the app again, your settings come back.

When the app is asked to delete your shop data, it deletes everything.

The app never replaces Shopify checkout and takes no payments.

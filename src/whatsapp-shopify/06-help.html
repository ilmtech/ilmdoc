Help

6.1 How it works

Shoppers tap the chat button or a WhatsApp button. Their own WhatsApp opens with a ready message to your number. They press Send and you reply from WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business as usual. No Meta account or API is needed.

6.2 Placeholders in messages

  • {product_title}, {variant}, {quantity}, {price}, {product_url}: product pages.
  • {cart_lines}, {cart_total}, {cart_link}, {cart_count}: cart. The link rebuilds the same cart for you.
  • {page_url}, {shop_name}: everywhere.

6.3 The widget does not show

  • Check that "ILM WhatsApp chat" is on in Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds, and saved.
  • The chat widget needs at least one active agent with a WhatsApp number and the switch on the Chat widget page.
  • Check Business hours (Hide the widget outside hours) and Where it shows (pages, devices, links).

6.4 Privacy

The app stores only your settings and anonymous click counts. It does not store shopper names, numbers or messages.

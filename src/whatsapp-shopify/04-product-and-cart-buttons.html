Product and cart buttons

4.1 Where the buttons show

Product buttons appear where you add the "ILM WhatsApp buttons" block on the product page. The cart button needs the app embed.

4.2 Order on WhatsApp on product pages

Turn on Show Order on WhatsApp on product pages. It sends the chosen variant, quantity and price to you.

  • Button text: up to 60 characters.
  • Message: up to 1000 characters. You can use {product_title}, {variant}, {quantity}, {price}, {product_url} and {shop_name}.

4.3 Order on WhatsApp in the cart

Turn on Show Order on WhatsApp in the cart. The button shows under the checkout button on the cart page and in cart drawers. It sends every item, the total and a link that rebuilds the cart.

  • Button text: up to 60 characters.
  • Message: up to 1000 characters. You can use {cart_lines}, {cart_total}, {cart_link}, {cart_count} and {shop_name}.
Tip The app never replaces Shopify checkout and takes no payments. Order on WhatsApp only sends an order request message. The cart link rebuilds the cart and leads to the normal Shopify checkout.

4.4 Ask about this product

Turn on Show Ask about this product. Shoppers can ask you a question about the product.

  • Button text: up to 60 characters.
  • Message: up to 1000 characters.
  • Show the icon: show the WhatsApp icon on the button.
  • Theme button class (optional): for example button button--secondary. Uses your theme's style instead of ours.
  • Choose collections: show the button only on products in these collections.

4.5 Share on WhatsApp

Turn on Show Share on WhatsApp. Shoppers send the product to their friends.

  • Button text: up to 60 characters.
  • Add the start of the product description: include the start of the description in the message.
  • Show the icon: show the WhatsApp icon on the button.
  • Theme button class (optional): uses your theme's style.
  • Choose collections: show the button only on products in these collections.

4.6 Subscribe on WhatsApp

Turn on Show Subscribe. Shoppers ask you for updates, or join your WhatsApp channel or group.

  • Button opens: A chat with my number, or A link (WhatsApp channel or group).
  • Link: the channel or group link.
  • Button text: up to 60 characters.
  • Message: up to 1000 characters.
  • Show the icon: show the WhatsApp icon on the button.
  • Theme button class (optional): uses your theme's style.
  • Choose collections: show the button only on products in these collections.

When you leave the WhatsApp number empty, the app uses your first active agent.

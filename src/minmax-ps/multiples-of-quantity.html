Multiples of QuantityCustomers must order in packs of this size, e.g. 3, 6, 9 with a value of 3.
Start from the minimumPart of Multiplies of Quantity. Enabled: steps count from the Minimum Quantity, so a minimum of 5 with steps of 2 allows 5, 7, 9… Disabled: 6, 8, 10 (multiples of 2 from the minimum up).

On the AI Assistant tab, describe your limits in your own words under Create rules with AI and click Build rules. The AI answers with a preview: one row per rule, with a note. Nothing is saved until you click Apply selected, and only ticked rows are saved. The rules then appear on the Quantity / Amount Rule tab like any other rule.

Write one limit per sentence and use the names of your categories, brands, suppliers and customer groups.

The AI features use AI answers of your AI plan: one answer for each Build rules, each Write with AI language and each Get an idea from AI. Check my rules uses one only when it finds a problem to explain. Set up a rule step by step is free and needs no AI plan. The line under Create rules with AI shows how many answers are left.

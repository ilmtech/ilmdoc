Uninstall

9.1 Remove the plugin

When you uninstall the plugin, its settings and statistics are removed. On a multisite network this happens on every site.

  1. Go to Plugins in your WordPress admin.
  2. Deactivate ILM Chat & Order for WhatsApp.
  3. Click Delete.
Tip Deleting the plugin removes your settings and click statistics. There is no undo, so export anything you want to keep first.

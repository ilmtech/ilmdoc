Cart order

7.1 Order from the cart

This button sits under Proceed to checkout on the cart page. The message lists every product with its options, quantity and price, and the cart total. It works with the classic cart and the Cart block.

  • Show an Order on WhatsApp button under Proceed to checkout: turns the button on or off.
  • WhatsApp number for orders: leave it empty to use the first agent.
  • Button text: the text on the button.
  • Message: the text sent with the cart order.

You can use these codes in the message: {cart_lines} {cart_total} {cart_count} {shop_name}.

Business hours

4.1 Set your hours

Business hours decide when your agents show as Online. The times use the site time zone, which is shown on the tab.

  • Use business hours: turns business hours on or off. Agents with their own hours use those instead.
  1. Tick Open for each day you work.
  2. Set From and To for the first period.
  3. If you close for a break, set a Second period.
  4. Click Save.

4.2 When nobody is online

  • When nobody is online: choose Show the widget with a "we reply soon" note, or Hide the widget.
  • Offline note: the text shown when the widget stays open outside your hours.

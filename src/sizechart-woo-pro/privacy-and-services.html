This chapter explains which external services the Pro add-on uses and what is sent to them. Nothing about customers or orders is sent to any of them.

ILM Tech licence service

  • When: activating the licence, moving it, and a daily check.
  • Sent: the order number at activation, the licence key and the site address.

ILM Tech AI service

  • When: when you use the AI builder.
  • Sent: the text you typed, the picture you chose, the chart being edited, and earlier messages of the same AI conversation.
  • Log: a log line per request, without the text or picture.
  • Free-plan requests only: the text and answer are kept without licence, site or IP, to improve the service.
  • Paid-plan requests: the text is not kept.

Your own AI provider

If you use your own key, the request goes directly to that provider with your key.

Find my size

Find my size uses no external service.

When a product comes in packs, the app can show the price of one pack right next to the product price, so shoppers instantly understand what they are buying. Our Classic Cotton T-shirt comes in packs of three, and today we will see how the pack price message looks and how to control where it appears. You will learn to edit the message text and choose its position on the product page and in product lists.

  1. Here is the pack line on the product page, sitting above the price. It tells the shopper that the T-shirt is sold in packs of three and shows the pack price. This helps avoid surprises at checkout and makes bulk buying clear.

  2. In Configuration, open the Advanced page. Under Multiple Quantity Configuration, you can change the text of this message. The field shown here lets you customize the wording, so you can say something like pack of three or bundle price in your own words.

  3. Product Position decides where the message goes on the product page. You enter the CSS selector of your theme price element, then choose before or after it. Here we click A I, which means after the element, so the pack line will appear below the price.

  4. Now the pack line is under the price. Shoppers see the pack price right below the regular price, making the offer clear at a glance. This is exactly what we set with the after position.

  5. Product List Position does the same on collection pages. You can set a selector and before or after there too. Switch either one off to hide the message on that page type.

ILM All In One Captcha - Anti-Spam stops spam and fake accounts on your PrestaShop forms. Every answer is checked on your server, so a bot cannot skip the check by turning JavaScript off or by sending the form directly. You can use the built-in ILM Captcha (no keys, no outside service), a math, text or icon picture, or Cloudflare Turnstile, Google reCAPTCHA and hCaptcha. Hidden checks, e-mail and text filters, IP and country rules, rate limits and e-mail confirmation of new accounts add more layers. This guide follows the module tabs.

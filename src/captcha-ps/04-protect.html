Block visitors, emails and words

4.1 The Protect tab

Open the Protect tab to stop unwanted visitors and messages before they reach your forms.

4.2 Customer and IP settings

  • Disable Validation for Customers: logged in customers do not see the captcha.
  • Whitelist IPs: one IP address per line. Visitors from these addresses are not checked. Click Add my IP to add your own address.
  • Block IPs: one IP address per line. Visitors from these addresses are blocked.

4.3 Email settings

  • Ban Disposable Emails: blocks email addresses from known spam providers. The spam provider list link shows the list.
  • Ban Email Addresses: one email per line. These addresses are blocked.
  • Block Email Domains: one email domain per line, for example dummy.com.

4.4 Message words

  • Block Message contains Text: words separated by commas. A message that contains one of these words is blocked.
Tip Click Save at the bottom of the tab to keep your changes.
